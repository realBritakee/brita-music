# DESIGN: Brita Music

This app uses the look of the DMZ site (`G:/Projects/DMZ/site/src/styles`). It wins over the general Brita look.

- Colours: greys from `#1B1B1B` to `#4A4A4A`, one accent orange `#FF6F00`. Status colours only for status. Tokens are in `src/renderer/src/styles/theme.css`.
- Shapes: square corners (2 px), 1 px borders, no soft shadows.
- Type: Saira for everything, bundled locally. Headings, navigation and buttons in uppercase. Regular weight for normal text, bold for titles.
- Icons: Lucide line icons.
- Dark only.
- Labels above inputs, plain words in all user-facing text.
